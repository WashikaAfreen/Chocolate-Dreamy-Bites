Legal & Information Centre

Here you'll find all our policies, terms, and frequently asked questions in one place.

Privacy Policy

We take your privacy seriously. Here's how we protect your information:

Your data is used solely to process and deliver your order. We will never sell or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Terms & Conditions

Products

Pricing & Payment
Prices are in GBP (£) and include VAT. Payment is required before items are dispatched.

Delivery
Delivery typically takes 3–5 working days within the UK. We are not responsible for delays caused by the courier.

Returns
Due to food safety regulations, perishable goods cannot be returned unless faulty or damaged on arrival. Contact us within 48 hours if your order arrives damaged.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?
Email us your product names and quantities, plus your full delivery address. We'll confirm your order and send payment details.

How much is delivery?
Free delivery on all orders over £15. Orders under £15 have a flat delivery fee of £3.00 (UK only).

Do your products contain allergens?
All our products contain milk and may contain traces of nuts. Full ingredient lists are available on the Products page.

How long does delivery take?
Orders are usually dispatched within 2 working days and delivered within 3–5 days.
